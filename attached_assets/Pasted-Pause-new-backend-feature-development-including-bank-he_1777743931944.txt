Pause new backend feature development (including bank health monitoring).

We now need to focus on **end-to-end product readiness and UX validation**.

---

# 🎯 OBJECTIVE

Validate that a real user can:

1. Sign up
2. Connect bank (mock or real)
3. Connect Gmail
4. See accurate subscriptions
5. Understand *why* each subscription exists
6. Understand savings opportunities
7. Reach a point where the product feels worth paying for (£4/month)

---

# 🧪 REQUIRED END-TO-END TEST SCENARIOS

## Flow 1: First-time user (bank only)

* Onboarding → bank connection → sync
* Validate:

  * subscriptions load correctly
  * dashboard is understandable without Gmail
  * insights are clear and not overwhelming

---

## Flow 2: Gmail-only user

* Gmail connect → scan → results
* Validate:

  * subscriptions still feel meaningful without bank data
  * explanations are understandable

---

## Flow 3: Combined power user (critical)

* Bank + Gmail connected
* Validate:

  * merged subscriptions make intuitive sense
  * audit trail explains merges clearly
  * no cognitive overload
  * totals match expectations visually and conceptually

---

# 🧠 UX VALIDATION REQUIREMENTS

For every screen, verify:

## 1. Clarity

* Can a non-technical user understand what they are seeing?

## 2. Trust

* Does the user understand why numbers are correct?

## 3. Actionability

* Does the UI clearly suggest what to cancel?

---

# 📊 PRODUCT QUESTIONS TO ANSWER

* Do users immediately understand “where their money is going”?
* Do insights feel actionable or just informational?
* Is there a clear “save money” moment within 30 seconds of onboarding?

---

# ⚠️ IMPORTANT

* Do NOT add new features during this phase
* Do NOT modify core backend logic unless bugs are found
* This is a **product validation phase, not a build phase**

---

# 🚀 OUTPUT REQUIRED

Return:

* UX issues found
* Confusing screens
* Missing clarity points
* Any trust gaps in explanations
* Any mismatches between data and user perception

---

After this, we will proceed to final phase: **monetization + production launch readiness**.
