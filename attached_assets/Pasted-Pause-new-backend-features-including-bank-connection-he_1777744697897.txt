Pause new backend features (including bank connection health monitoring).

We now need to improve **first-session onboarding and immediate value discovery**.

---

# 🎯 OBJECTIVE

Design a guided onboarding flow that ensures users experience **clear financial value within 60 seconds of signup**.

---

# 🧠 CORE PROBLEM

Right now users:

* sign up
* land on dashboard
* may or may not connect bank/Gmail immediately
* do not feel urgency or payoff

We need to fix this.

---

# 🚀 REQUIRED FLOW

## STEP 1 — Onboarding Landing State (NEW)

After signup, show a **guided setup screen**, not dashboard.

### Must include:

### “Let’s find your hidden subscriptions”

With 2 primary actions:

* Connect Bank (primary CTA)
* Connect Gmail (secondary CTA)

---

## STEP 2 — VALUE PREVIEW MODE (IMPORTANT)

Before full connection, show a **mock preview card**:

Example:

> “People like you typically spend £48–£120/month on unused subscriptions”

This is NOT real user data — it’s onboarding context.

---

## STEP 3 — FIRST CONNECTION MOMENT

After first bank or Gmail sync:

Immediately show:

### “We found your subscriptions”

And highlight:

* total monthly spend
* top 3 subscriptions
* biggest single cost

This must be the **first emotional payoff screen**

---

## STEP 4 — AUTOMATIC INSIGHT TRIGGER

On first sync:

* automatically run analysis
* show results instantly (no button required)
* highlight “potential savings”

---

## STEP 5 — DASHBOARD ENTRY POINT

Only AFTER onboarding:

→ user enters full dashboard

---

# 🎨 UX REQUIREMENTS

* No empty dashboards after signup
* No manual “Run Analysis” dependency
* Clear progression:
  Signup → Connect → Insight → Dashboard

---

# ⚠️ IMPORTANT RULES

* Do NOT remove existing systems
* Do NOT change backend logic
* This is purely a **UX + flow layer**
* Must preserve current APIs

---

# 🚀 OUTCOME

User should experience:

> “Oh wow, I didn’t realise I was spending this much”
> within the first minute
