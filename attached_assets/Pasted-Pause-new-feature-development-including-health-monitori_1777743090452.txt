Pause new feature development (including health monitoring).

We now need to add a **Financial Audit & Explanation Layer** to make all calculations transparent and user-verifiable.

---

# 🎯 OBJECTIVE

Make every financial output in SubTrack **fully explainable to the user**.

Users must be able to understand:

* Why a subscription exists
* How it was detected
* Why it was merged or not merged
* How monthly totals are calculated

---

# 🧠 CORE FEATURE: AUDIT TRAIL

For every subscription, store and expose:

## 1. Detection history

* source events (bank / email)
* timestamps of detection
* matching logic used (fuzzy match, amount match, etc.)

---

## 2. Merge explanation

If a subscription was merged:

* “Merged with existing subscription due to merchant similarity + amount match”

If NOT merged:

* “No matching subscription found within confidence threshold”

---

## 3. Confidence breakdown

Expose:

* bank_score
* email_score
* final_confidence
* boost factors applied

---
